How the scores work

  • Price (25%) — half how cheap the estate is per sqm against the median of its own corridor, half how cheap it is across Abuja overall (log scale, ₦5,000/sqm = 100, ₦300,000/sqm = 0). Where a developer charges infrastructure or development fees on top, they are added in.
  • Title (25%) — the title the seller claims: FCDA C of O with approved plan 95, FCDA C of O 90, Governor's consent or FCDA allocation with building plan 85, R of O 75, C of O with issuer unclear 65–70, area-council/customary 45, none stated 25. None of these were verified at AGIS.
  • Infrastructure (25%) — how ready the estate is for you to build within 1–3 years: residents already living there, tarred roads, fence, power and water score high; advertised future amenities score low.
  • Access (15%) — approximate off-peak drive to the Central Area; 10 minutes scores 100, each extra minute costs 1.3 points.
  • Stale prices — where the price is undated or more than nine months old, the price score is capped at 50, because today's price is unknown.
  • Nasarawa titles — a NAGIS (Nasarawa) C of O scores like an FCDA C of O with a building plan (85); a global R of O with a deed of assignment scores 70; a deed and survey alone score 35.
  • Price transparency (10%) — recent, consistent prices with no hidden fees score high; expired promos, undated lists and agents quoting different prices score low.
  • Grades — A 68+, B 60–67, C 50–59, D under 50. Estates with no current published price are mapped but not rated.
  • Before paying anywhere — run an AGIS search (FCT land) or a NAGIS search (Nasarawa land) on the estate's global title file number, confirm the plot sits inside it with your own surveyor, and get the full fee schedule (infrastructure, development levy, survey, legal, agency 5%) in writing.

Prices shown are public asking prices, not a valuation or an offer. Titles are what sellers claim and have not been verified. Nothing here is legal, surveying, or investment advice.
