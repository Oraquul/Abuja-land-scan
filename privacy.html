Disclaimer and privacy notice

This notice covers the Abuja Estate Land Scan, the Lagos + Ogun Land Scan, and the Plot-to-Home Studio on each edition. It is written with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) in mind.

Disclaimer

  • Prices are public asking prices taken from listings and published posts. They are not a valuation, an offer, or the price a plot will sell for.
  • Titles are what the seller claims. They have not been verified at AGIS (FCT) or NAGIS (Nasarawa).
  • Map pins are approximate to the area, not plot boundaries.
  • Nothing on this site is legal, surveying, tax, or investment advice. Search the title and visit the site with your own lawyer and surveyor before you pay.

Who is responsible

The operator of this website decides why enquiry details are used. This page does not name a person. Use the enquiry form on the land scan, or the WhatsApp button when it is shown, and say that the message is a data-protection request.

What the enquiry form collects

If you send the form, it collects your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, the estate you are interested in, a budget band, whether you are a local buyer, a diaspora buyer, or a developer, which edition the form was sent from (Abuja or Lagos-Ogun), and the fact that you ticked the consent box. You do not have to send the form to read the site.

The form is for adults. Do not send it about a child or with a child’s details.

Why it is collected

The details are used only to reply to your enquiry. The lawful basis is your consent. The consent box is unchecked until you tick it, and you can withdraw consent later by asking for your details to be deleted. Withdrawing consent does not affect a reply that was already sent.

Where it goes

The form sends your message to the form service the operator has configured, such as Formspree or Web3Forms. It is not written into this website’s files. Those files are public, so enquiry messages must never be copied into the repository.

The form service may store or transmit the message outside Nigeria. Sending the form is your consent to that transfer for the purpose of receiving a reply. The operator should keep the provider’s own terms and security measures in mind, and should delete messages when they are no longer needed.

Plot-to-Home Studio sessions stay in the browser you used, unless you download a backup. That backup is on your own device. Do not commit it to this repository.

WhatsApp

The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with a draft that names the estate on the screen. It does not include your name, phone, or email. The conversation is with the number the operator configured, on WhatsApp’s own service. This site does not receive the chat. If no number has been configured, the button is not shown.

Analytics

Analytics are off unless the operator turns them on. When on, the site loads GoatCounter or Plausible, which are built to avoid advertising cookies and cross-site profiles. The events are a page view, an enquiry sent (without the contents of the form), a WhatsApp click, and an estate scorecard view (the estate name only). Your name, phone, email, budget, and buyer type are not sent to analytics.

How long it is kept

Enquiry details should be kept only long enough to reply and for a short follow-up, then deleted. Ask for deletion sooner if you want that, and the operator should pass the request to the form service as well.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask for a copy of your personal data, ask for a correction, ask for deletion, withdraw consent, and object to a use that is no longer necessary to reply to you. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated page is published at this address. The form will still ask for consent before anything is sent.
